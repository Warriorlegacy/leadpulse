# LeadPulse — Market Research

Status: honest scoping note. **No primary buyer interviews, no surveys, and no paid market data were obtained.** Everything below is a hypothesis used to frame the product, not a research finding. Any claim about a third party is labelled `unverified`.

## 1. What was not done

- No buyer or user interviews.
- No paid analyst reports, market-size datasets, or pricing databases.
- No landing-page demand test, ad spend test, or willingness-to-pay study.
- No competitor pricing verification.
- Consequently: no TAM/SAM/SOM, no growth rates, no conversion benchmarks, no customer counts, and no price points appear in this or any linked document.

## 2. Buyer hypothesis (unvalidated)

- Segment: 2–25 person marketing agencies and local service businesses ([PRD.md](PRD.md)).
- Assumption: they buy leads through ads/forms, have no RevOps function, and lose enquiries to slow or unowned follow-up.
- Assumption: one person (owner or ops lead) both buys and uses the tool.
- These are the same assumptions recorded in [ASSUMPTIONS.md](ASSUMPTIONS.md). They have not been confirmed with a single buyer.

## 3. Category context only

## 4. Differentiation hypothesis (unvalidated)

- Narrow wedge: **response-time discipline plus source attribution for small teams** — i.e. making "who owns this lead, what is the next action, and how long did we take" observable, rather than offering a broad suite.
- This is a positioning hypothesis, not a proven gap. It is `unverified` whether existing tools already cover it adequately, and `unverified` whether buyers will pay for it standalone.
- Consistent with the core promise in [PRD.md](PRD.md) and the metrics in [METRIC_DEFINITIONS.md](METRIC_DEFINITIONS.md).

## 5. Pricing

- INR pricing is an unvalidated hypothesis ([ASSUMPTIONS.md](ASSUMPTIONS.md)). No price, tier, or currency claim is made here.
- No competitor pricing is quoted; all `unverified`.

## 6. What would invalidate the thesis

- Buyers do not track or care about response time.
- Ownership is already enforced well enough by their current tools or habits.
- The segment prefers an all-in-one suite over a narrow tool.
- Buyers will not pay without outbound automation, which is an explicit MVP non-goal ([PRD.md](PRD.md)).
